How to play

  • ●Tap your planet to select it. Hold another of your planets to add it too. Tap a selected planet to deselect it.
  • ➤With planets selected, tap any other planet to send units: enemy or grey planets get attacked, your own get reinforced. The selection clears after each send.
  • !When a planet is under attack it flashes red with the incoming count. Tap its red DEFEND badge to pull just enough units from your nearby planets.
  • ↗Or drag from your planet straight to any planet to send in one move.
  • ◯Draw a loop on empty space to select several planets. Tap empty space to deselect all.
  • ●The three little bars on top of each planet show its level (1–3). A faint ring around your planet means it can still be upgraded; when it brightens a ▲ badge pops up next to it: tap the badge to upgrade at once (no need to select the planet). A gold rim with three gold bars = fully upgraded.
  • ▲With one planet selected, tap Upgrade to spend its orbiting units on a level-up (max 3).
  • ◆Rivals mass before they strike: a swarm gathering in open space near your planet means an attack is coming from that side. Reinforce it, or hit the swarm first. Red attacks often and follows up, picking on the weakest. Green turtles and punishes attackers who just lost a fight. Violet feints, threatening one planet and hitting another. Orange grabs neutrals two at a time. Pink masses out of sight to snipe cores, factories and drained planets, and backs off if spotted.
  • %The % button sets how much each planet sends. Destroy every rival planet and unit to win.
  • ↩Change your mind: tap one of your moving fleets (or draw a loop around part of it) to select it, then tap another planet to redirect it, or empty space to park it there. ↩ Recall sends it back to your nearest planet.
  • ◎ALL (or A) selects every planet, swarm and moving fleet you own; the next tap sends from planets at your send % and redirects all fleets.
  • ?Hold a special planet (or hover it with a mouse) to see what it does.
  • ◌Swarms: with something selected, tap empty space (or drag there) to park units as a hovering swarm. Swarms don't produce, and they fight any enemy they touch. Tap a swarm to select it, then tap a planet or another spot to move it. Drag back onto the start to cancel.
  • ✦Feature sectors (marked ✦) add terrain: asteroid walls, nebulae, slingshot lanes, solar flares, special planets and objectives. A tip explains each one the first time you meet it.
  • ⚄Skirmish builds a fresh random map: pick size, rivals, terrain and start, or tap Play random. Up to 5 rivals, including Orange (expansionist) and Pink (sniper). Turn on Teams to fight with AI allies. Type a seed to replay a map. Endless keeps going after each win with tougher rivals and counts your streak.
  • ⇄Multiplayer: one phone taps Create room and shares the 4-letter code or link; friends tap Join. Up to 6 players: colors are handed out at random (tap a free color to switch; the host can shuffle) and spawns are shuffled each match. Pick a handmade map or 🎲 Random map (same seed = same map for everyone). Teams: teammates can't hurt each other, can reinforce each other's planets, and share vision. Everyone taps Ready, the host starts. Your color is shown at the top and your home is always at the bottom.
