How to play

  • ●Tap your planet to select it. Tap it again to deselect. Tap more of your planets to add them.
  • ➤With planets selected, tap an enemy or grey planet to send units. The selection clears after each send.
  • ↗Drag from your planet to any planet to send in one move. This is also how you reinforce your own planets.
  • ◯Draw a loop on empty space to select several planets. Tap empty space to deselect all.
  • ▲With one planet selected, tap Upgrade to spend its orbiting units on a level-up (max 3).
  • %The % button sets how much each planet sends. Destroy every rival planet and unit to win.
